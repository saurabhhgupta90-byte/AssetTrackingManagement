# Asset Tracking Management System

## Overview

The Asset Tracking Management System is a Salesforce application developed to manage and monitor organizational assets throughout their lifecycle. The system helps organizations track asset allocation, maintenance schedules, warranty information, and asset status while improving operational efficiency through Salesforce automation.

## Features

- Asset Registration and Management
- Asset Allocation to Employees
- Asset Return Management
- Asset Maintenance Tracking
- Warranty Expiry Monitoring
- Asset Status Management
- Automated Approval Process
- Email Notifications
- Reports and Dashboards
- Role-Based Security
- Validation Rules
- Record-Triggered Flows
- Apex Triggers
- Lightning Record Pages
- Custom Objects and Relationships
